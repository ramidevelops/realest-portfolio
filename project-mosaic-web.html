The Problem

Mosaic's web presence had grown into a sprawling, inconsistent collection of sites — hurting the brand more than helping it. Visitors rarely made it past the homepage, most traffic arrived cold through Google search rather than site navigation, and articles averaged just 8 seconds of read time. The information architecture buried content too deep to find, undermining the premium feel a Fortune 500 company needs.

79.4% Average bounce rate per month

10+ Fragmented web experiences
2 Focused sites after consolidation
Before

The existing site had no cohesive visual language across properties — inconsistent typography, photography treatment, and navigation patterns made it feel like multiple companies, not one Fortune 500 brand.

Approach

I directed the project end-to-end in partnership with iCrossing — running stakeholder interviews to align both teams, then owning content strategy, information architecture, and visual direction for two consolidated properties: a corporate site and a South America marketing site.

Add image — South America marketing site
Design Direction

A unified visual system — consistent photography treatment, typography hierarchy, and component language — applied across both properties to establish a coherent brand presence at scale.

Status

Design direction fully approved by leadership and moved into development. Project currently paused due to budget conditions — build-ready assets on the shelf.
