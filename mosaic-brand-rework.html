Mosaic Brand Guidelines

Design Lead — Adopted Across 13,000 Employees
Mosaic
BrandSystem

As Mosaic's sole design lead, reworked the company's brand guidelines — originally built by an outside vendor — to close the gaps that surfaced after years of real-world use across a Fortune 500 organization.

Add image — Before: original guideline spread

The original system was too rigid to scale: logo sizing rules were tied to a handful of static examples with no underlying grid system, colors lacked any guidance on visual balance despite a broad palette, and large-format applications — billboards, backdrops, pull-up banners, formats Mosaic produces constantly — were never addressed at all.

Add image — Before: logo scaling issue
Add image — After: logo scaling issue resolved

I rebuilt the logo system around percentage-based sizing tied to canvas width, replacing the old static-example approach with a rule that scales cohesively across any format while preserving the brand recognition a Fortune 500 company needs. I also added visual balance guidance for color usage, built out how-to guides and real-world layout examples, and mocked up large-format applications that hadn't previously been covered.

Every decision was grounded in who Mosaic actually is — the world's top phosphate and potash producer — a serious, bold brand, not a bland or playful one.

Beyond the system itself, I coordinated Spanish translations for South America and led the internal rollout — building the communications plan and updating every template company-wide. The refreshed guidelines are now adopted across all 13,000 employees, giving teams a clearer, more confident grasp of the brand and how to apply it.
