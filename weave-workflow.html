Figma Weave AI Workflow

Solo Build — 2 Days to 4 Hours Turnaround
Mosaic
AISystem

Built a Figma Weave AI workflow to eliminate the friction around producing simple social assets — what used to require sourcing images, sourcing copy, and looping in multiple collaborators just to ship one Instagram post.

The workflow ingests the social calendar brief and converts it into structured JSON — headline, content, and call-to-action — then automatically categorizes each post (community investment, operations, recruiting, and more) to determine the right background treatment for that category.

I fed the system Mosaic's brand guidelines and style rules, plus a library of approved reference assets, so every generated output stayed on-brand without manual oversight.

Built and maintained solo, the workflow cut asset turnaround from 2 days to 4 hours — freeing up time for higher-priority design work instead of repetitive production.
